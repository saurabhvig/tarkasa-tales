# 30-day post-launch marketing plan

This folder is not part of the website build. Nothing here appears on tarkasatales.com.

**Goals:** organic visitors, Learning Club members, Amazon clicks, and real book sales (KDP).
**Budget:** €0 in ads (as decided); about 3–4 hours a week.
**Tools:** Instagram, YouTube, Canva, Brevo, Umami, Search Console and KDP Reports.

**Rule for every post:** link to a **page on tarkasatales.com**, not straight to Amazon, using the campaign links in `utm-links.md`. The website shows every country's store, counts the click, and invites people to the Learning Club.

## Week 0: baseline (launch day)

Note today's numbers in a simple table. Without them you can't see growth.

- Umami: visitors per week, `amazon-click`, `download`
- Brevo: Learning Club members
- KDP: units sold in the last 30 days, per book

## Week 1: launch and free printables

- [ ] **Instagram:** 1 reel, "6 free printables for curious kids 4–8". Show the calm-down cards and the observation journal being used. Link in bio → `/activities/` (bio link with UTM). Story with a link sticker to the same page.
- [ ] **Instagram:** 1 carousel, "Bobbin's 8 calm-down ideas" (5 slides). Last slide: "Free printable: link in bio".
- [ ] **Learning Club:** switch on the welcome automation (after approval).
- [ ] **Personal network:** a WhatsApp or LinkedIn message to 10–15 parents and teachers you know, with the `/activities/` link. Ask them to share it if it's useful.
- [ ] **Search:** Search Console and Bing set up; sitemap submitted.

## Week 2: Women in Science and the omnibus

- [ ] **Reel:** "3 girls who asked big questions": Marie, Jane, Katherine. Link → `/editions/women-who-asked-big-questions/` ("Hear first when it's ready" signup).
- [ ] **YouTube:** add the right book-page UTM link to the description of every existing book video. This is a one-off, about 30 minutes.
- [ ] **Teachers:** email 5 English-language primary schools or international schools near you (Rotterdam, The Hague) and 2 libraries. Offer the free reading guides and printables for class use. Attach nothing; give one link to `/activities/`.
- [ ] **First newsletter:** send in the first half of December (after approval).

## Week 3: gifts season (Sinterklaas and Christmas)

- [ ] **Reel or carousel:** "Gift ideas for curious kids 4–8". Link → `/gifts/`.
- [ ] **Pinterest (free, optional):** create a business account. Pin the 6 new printables, with the printable preview as the image and the Free Resources page as the link. Printables do well on Pinterest, and pins keep bringing visitors for months.
- [ ] **Reviews:** ask 5 families who own a book to leave an honest Amazon review (book page → "Review this book"). Never offer anything in return; Amazon forbids it.

**Reading the table:**

- Amazon clicks rising while KDP units stay flat? Visitors are interested but not buying. Check prices and reviews on Amazon.
- Downloads rising but no new members? Make the Learning Club invitation clearer.
- Units rising with no Amazon clicks from us? The sales came from elsewhere (e.g. Amazon search).

**Never** treat clicks as sales; only KDP Reports (and Amazon Attribution, where available) show sales.

## Starting targets (adjust after week 0)

These are deliberately small, to judge the plan, not promises:

- 5–10 new Learning Club members in the first month
- 1 printable download a day by week 4
- every book page reached at least once from search by day 30
