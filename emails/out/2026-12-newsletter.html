Science fact

What happens if you drop a hammer and a feather on the Moon?

They land at the same time! In 1971, astronaut David Scott tried it on the Moon during the Apollo 15 mission. On Earth, air slows the light, fluffy feather down. The Moon has no air, so gravity pulls both down together.

Try it together: hold a book and a small ball at the same height, over a soft rug, and let go at the same time. Then try a flat sheet of paper and a scrunched-up ball of paper. What changed?
