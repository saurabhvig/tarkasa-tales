Katherine Johnson

Count It, Check It: a Space Activity

Katherine counted everything: the steps to the road, the steps up to church, every fork and spoon she washed. Later, her careful numbers helped astronaut John Glenn fly around the Earth and home again.

Count it. Check it. Check again!

1Count the stars

Count the stars in each box and write the number. Then ask someone to check your count!

2Ready for launch!

Fill in the missing numbers. Then count down out loud: 10, 9, 8 … lift off!

108531

For grown-ups: in the book, Katherine checked every number by hand before the flight. Celebrate checking as much as the right answer: “You counted 7, I counted 7. Checked!”
