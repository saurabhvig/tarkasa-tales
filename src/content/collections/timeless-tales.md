Long before books, people told stories: of brave heroes, clever animals, wise teachers and gods and goddesses. Many of these stories have been passed from grandparents to grandchildren for thousands of years.

**Timeless Tales** retells some of these classic stories for young readers, with gentle language and warm, colourful illustrations. Each retelling is age-appropriate and written with care and respect for the traditions the stories come from, so they can be enjoyed both by families who already know them and by children hearing them for the first time.
