Every great discovery begins with a question. **Little Scientists** introduces young readers to real scientists through warm, beautifully illustrated storytelling, starting with the child each scientist once was.

These are true stories. The big events in every book really happened; some of the things people say are imagined, to help tell the story, and each book explains this on its first pages. Every science book is researched from reliable sources and checked by a biomedical scientist.
