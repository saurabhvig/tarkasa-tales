Some days, feelings are bigger than we are. In **Bobbin's World**, a small fox and her friends meet the everyday moments that make tummies flip, faces go hot and eyes prickle, and find out, without lecturing, what helps.

The stories are made for reading aloud and talking about together, with little questions along the way that invite children to guess what happens next.
