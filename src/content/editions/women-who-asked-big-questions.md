Marie wondered what was inside a stone. Jane waited hours to see where eggs come from. Katherine counted everything she could see. All three grew up to change science.

*Women Who Asked Big Questions* brings their three Little Scientists picture books together in one edition, for reading aloud over three evenings or sharing at school. Each story starts with the curious girl, not the famous grown-up, so young children can see themselves in her.

Until the edition is published, each story is available now as its own book.
