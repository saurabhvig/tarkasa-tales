Every great discovery begins with a question. **Big Questions** introduces young readers to real scientists through warm, beautifully illustrated storytelling — starting with the child each scientist once was.

These are true stories. The big events in every book really happened; some of the things people say are imagined, to help tell the story, and each book explains this on its first pages.

Every book ends with extras for reading together:

- **For grown-ups** — a short biography and timeline, with the sources used
- **Words to know** — a simple picture-book glossary
- **Be a curious scientist** — easy activities to try at home with a grown-up
- **My big question** — a page for your child to write and draw their own question

The series encourages curiosity, persistence, imagination and the habit of checking ideas — the way real scientists do.
