Every great discovery begins with a question. **Little Scientists** introduces young readers to real scientists through accessible, beautifully illustrated storytelling.

Each book follows a real person’s journey — their curiosity, their hard work and the challenges they met along the way — and explains their discoveries in words that young children can understand.

The series encourages:

- **Curiosity** — asking questions about the world
- **Persistence** — keeping going when things are difficult
- **Scientific thinking** — observing, testing and learning
- **Creativity** — imagining new possibilities
- **Learning from challenges** — seeing mistakes as part of discovery

Our biographies are based on the historical record. We do not invent events, quotations or discoveries.
