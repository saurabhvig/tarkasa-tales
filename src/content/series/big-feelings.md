Some days, feelings are bigger than we are. **Big Feelings** stories follow gentle animal friends through the everyday moments that make tummies flip, faces go hot and eyes prickle — and show, without lecturing, what helps.

The stories are made for reading aloud and talking about together, with little questions along the way that invite children to guess what happens next.
