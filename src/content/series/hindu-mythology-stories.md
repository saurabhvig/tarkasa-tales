For thousands of years, families have shared the stories of Hindu tradition from one generation to the next. **Hindu Mythology Stories** retells some of these traditional tales for young readers, with gentle language and colourful illustrations.

Each book is an age-appropriate retelling, written with care and respect for the traditions the stories come from. They can be enjoyed by families who know these stories well and by anyone discovering them for the first time.
