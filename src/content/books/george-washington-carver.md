Little George was too poorly to work in the fields, so he wandered in the woods instead. He looked closely at every leaf and flower, made a secret garden of his own, and became so good at helping droopy plants that his neighbours called him **the Plant Doctor**.

When the nearby school would not let him in, George set off on foot to find one that would. He kept learning, kept trying, and grew up to help farmers bring their tired soil back to life. He even built a *school on wheels* to carry seeds and lessons right to families’ doors.

A gentle, true story that shows young children how noticing, caring and sharing can change the world, with the easy-to-remember refrain **Notice. Care. Share.** It is perfect for little gardeners, nature lovers and any child who has ever asked *why a plant is drooping*.

**Inside you’ll also find:** a “For grown-ups” biography and timeline (with a note on the famous peanut myths), a picture glossary of words to know, simple plant experiments to try at home, and a page for your child’s own big question.
