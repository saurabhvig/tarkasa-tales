Isaac was so tiny when he was born that he could have fitted inside a big jug. But he grew up full of questions. He built a windmill run by a mouse, flew a kite with a lantern that made the neighbours gasp, and was a hopeless farmer because he was always reading.

Then one day an apple fell in the orchard. *Why does it always fall straight down? Could the same pull reach all the way to the Moon?* Isaac’s answer changed how we understand the whole universe.

A true story about noticing everyday things, asking questions and testing your ideas, with the easy-to-remember refrain **Look. Ask. Test.**

**Inside you’ll also find:** a “For grown-ups” biography and timeline, a picture glossary of words to know, simple experiments to try at home, and a page for your child’s own big question.
