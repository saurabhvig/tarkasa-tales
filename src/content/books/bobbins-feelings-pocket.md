Bobbin the fox is back, in her big yellow coat with its deep, useful pockets. This time there are **five big feelings** to meet, one in each story:

- **Angry:** Dabble bounces onto Old Stump and Bobbin’s Treasure Tower comes crashing down. Her ears go hot, her tail goes THUMP, and her paws push before she can think.
- **Worried:** Bobbin is asked to say a poem at the Harvest Gathering, and the croaky What-If Frogs move into her tummy.
- **Sad:** Moss the turtle goes to sleep for the whole winter, and a little grey cloud follows Bobbin around.
- **Scared:** on Dabble’s very first sleepover, a thunderstorm crashes and a monster shadow appears on the wall.
- **Jealous:** baby cousin Pip comes to stay, needs Nana a LOT, and prickles start sticking to Bobbin’s fur.

With Nana Fox’s calm help, Bobbin learns to say hello to each feeling, to ask **“How big is it? Acorn-small, apple-middle or pumpkin-BIG?”**, and to try simple tools such as Bun-and-Soup Breath, Stop Paws, Hug-and-Hold and the Brave Roar. After each story she keeps a small treasure in her pocket to remember what helped. By the end, her pocket is full.

Made for reading aloud: on later readings, teal prompts invite children to **Pause, Ask, Try it and Help Bobbin**. Each story ends with Bobbin’s Pocket Tools to practise together when everyone is calm, and the back of the book has notes for grown-ups on each feeling, with ideas and questions to talk about, plus a “My Feelings Pocket” page for your child to draw their own treasures.

These stories support everyday feelings skills. They are not a substitute for professional advice.
