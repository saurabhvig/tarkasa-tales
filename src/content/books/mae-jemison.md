When little Mae’s teacher asked what she wanted to be, Mae said, “A scientist.” “Don’t you mean a nurse?” asked the teacher. Mae put her hands on her hips. **“No. I mean a scientist.”**

Mae loved the stars, the library and dancing, and she didn’t have to choose between science and dance. She became an engineer and a doctor, and then, out of about two thousand people, NASA chose her to train as an astronaut. In 1992 she flew on the space shuttle *Endeavour* and became **the first Black woman to travel into space**, where she even looked after frog eggs that hatched into tadpoles!

A true, joyful story about big dreams, hard work and making room for every part of you, with the refrain **Dream big. Learn lots. Reach far.** Perfect for space-mad children, little dancers and anyone who has been told “that’s not for you”.

**Inside you’ll also find:** a “For grown-ups” biography and timeline, a picture glossary of words to know, four simple activities to try at home, and a page for your child to write and draw their own big dream.
