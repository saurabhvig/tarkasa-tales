Princess Sita has been carried far away to the island of Lanka, and a great band of monkeys and bears sets off to find her. But at the edge of the ocean they stop. Nobody can leap all the way across, and Hanuman sits quietly at the back, his tail drooping.

Then old Jambavan, the wise bear, reminds him: as a baby, Hanuman once leapt into the sky to grab the sun, thinking it was a juicy fruit! Hanuman remembers how strong he is. With a mighty **WHOOSH**, he leaps over the sea, thanks the golden mountain Mainaka, outwits the giant Surasa by shrinking as tiny as a thumb, and finds Sita in a moonlit garden to give her Rama’s ring.

A gentle, age-appropriate retelling of a favourite episode from the Ramayana, with the refrain **Be strong. Be clever. Be kind!** Told with care and respect, for families who already love the story and for children hearing it for the first time.

**Inside you’ll also find:** a pronunciation guide, a “For grown-ups: who’s who” page, a picture glossary of words to know, four simple activities to try at home, a “My turn” page for your child, and notes on where the story comes from.
