Long ago, King Bhagiratha of Ayodhya had one big wish: to bring the holy river Ganga down from the sky to the Earth. But a whole river, falling from the heavens? **SPLASH!** It would crash right through the ground. Who could ever catch it?

Bhagiratha prays, and waits, and even stands on the tip of one toe for a whole year. Wibble, wobble! At last Lord Shiva agrees to help. He catches the roaring river in his long, tangled hair, where Ganga swirls round and round like a maze. Then, with a little more patience and a surprise **GULP!** from the sage Jahnu, Ganga follows Bhagiratha’s chariot all the way to the sea.

A gentle, age-appropriate retelling of the story of Ganga’s descent from the Ramayana, with the refrain **Ask kindly. Wait patiently. Keep going!** Told with care and respect, for families who already love the story and for children hearing it for the first time.

**Inside you’ll also find:** a pronunciation guide, a “For grown-ups: who’s who” page, a picture glossary of words to know, four simple activities to try at home, a “My turn” page for your child, and notes on where the story comes from.
