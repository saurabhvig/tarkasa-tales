Stuck in bed one day, little Albert is given a compass. Every time he turns it, the needle swings back the same way. No finger pushes it. No string pulls it. *What’s moving it?*

That question never left him. Follow Albert from his violin lessons and school days to the patent office in Bern, where he dreamed up ideas that amazed the world — from tiny packets of light to the famous E = mc² — and to the eclipse that proved his idea right.

With the easy-to-remember refrain **Wonder. Imagine. Check!**, this true story shows children that big ideas start with wondering, grow with imagination, and become science when we check them.

**Inside you’ll also find:** a “For grown-ups” biography and timeline with sources (including a note on common Einstein myths), a picture glossary of words to know, four simple activities to try at home, and a “My big question” page for your child to fill in.
