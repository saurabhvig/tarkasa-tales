When four-year-old Jane wanted to know how a hen lays an egg, she curled up in a henhouse and waited for hours, until *plop!* She saw it with her own eyes.

Years later, in the forests of Gombe in Africa, Jane waited and watched again. Little by little, the wild chimpanzees learned to trust her, and one of them showed her something that changed science forever.

A true story about patience, curiosity and caring for animals, with the easy-to-remember refrain **Wait. Watch. Wonder.**

**Inside you’ll also find:** a “For grown-ups” biography and timeline with sources, a picture glossary of words to know, simple activities to try at home, and a page for your child’s own big question.
