Beside a sparkling lake called Pampa, a woman named Shabari lives in a quiet forest home. Before her teachers leave, they make her a promise: one day, Prince Rama will come. Welcome him.

So every morning, Shabari sweeps the path, swish, swish, swish, and picks the sweetest fruit she can find. Days turn into months, and months into years. Her hair turns white and a cheeky monkey wonders if Rama will *really* come. Then one day: crunch, crunch, crunch, footsteps on the path! Shabari welcomes Rama and Lakshmana with cool water and a basket of fruit, and Rama says no palace has ever welcomed them so well.

A gentle, age-appropriate retelling of a much-loved episode from the Ramayana, with the refrain **Sweep the path. Pick the fruit. Be ready!** Told with care and respect, for families who already love the story and for children hearing it for the first time.

**Inside you’ll also find:** a pronunciation guide, a “For grown-ups: who’s who” page, a picture glossary of words to know, four simple activities to try at home, a “My turn” page for your child, and notes on where the story comes from.
