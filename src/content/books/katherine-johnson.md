Katherine counted everything: the steps to the road, the steps up to church, every fork and spoon she washed. She was so quick at learning that she was ready for high school at ten.

When unfair rules kept her out of school, her family moved so she could learn. Years later, astronaut John Glenn trusted her to check the numbers that would fly him around the Earth and bring him safely home.

A true story about loving numbers, never giving up and the power of checking your work, with the easy-to-remember refrain **Count it. Check it. Check again!**

**Inside you’ll also find:** a “For grown-ups” biography and timeline, a picture glossary of words to know, simple activities to try at home, and a page for your child to record what they counted.
